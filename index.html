about me

Hi! I’m Candela Ribas, a graphic and audiovisual designer from Ibiza, Spain. I enjoy creating projects where aesthetics and functionality go hand in hand. I believe great design comes from observing the world and its details, experimenting without fear, and exploring new ideas.

I'm versatile and curious: I move freely between visual aesthetics and UX logic. I’m always looking to give each project its own personality and make an impact.

Thanks for checking out, feel free to explore!
